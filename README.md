# Modestox Reorder & Product Insights for Shopware 6

Language: **English** | [Deutsch](README.de.md) | [Русский](README.ru.md)

---

A Shopware 6 plugin that links products with orders, displays purchase history in Customer Profile and Admin Product View, and enables fast reordering in the Storefront.

## Features

- **Orders in Product View (Admin):** View all orders containing a specific product directly in the product details.
- **Purchased Products in Customer Profile (Admin):** See an aggregated list of all items bought by a customer.
- **My Purchased Products & Reorder (Storefront):** Allows customers to quickly repurchase previously ordered items from their account.

## Configuration

All features can be independently enabled or disabled via Plugin Settings in Administration:
- `Enable "Orders in Product View" (Admin)`
- `Enable "Purchased Products" in Customer Profile (Admin)`
- `Enable "My Purchased Products & Reorder" in Storefront Account`
